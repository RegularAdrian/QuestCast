Built for immediacy

A small, direct path from headset to television.

QuestCast favours the newest picture over a perfect delayed stream. Late or incomplete frames are discarded, keeping the experience responsive.

⌁

The direct route

Hardware capture to hardware decode.

QUESTLOCAL NETWORKAPPLE TV

Android MediaProjection and H.264 hardware encoding feed Apple’s VideoToolbox decoder, with no computer or internet service in the middle.

◉

Automatic discovery

Open both apps. Pick your TV.

Bonjour finds QuestCast TV on the local network, so there are no IP addresses or pairing codes to type.

◒

Audio on your terms

Playback audio is opt-in.

Enable experimental 48 kHz stereo audio manually on the headset. It stays off by default and never captures the microphone.

⌘

Useful when needed

Diagnostics without the clutter.

Press Play/Pause on the Siri Remote to reveal codec, frame, loss, and audio-buffer information over the live picture.

◇

Private by design

Your stream stays at home.

No account, analytics, cloud service, recording, or relay. Use QuestCast only on a trusted local network.
